Account Deletion

Request to permanently delete your Drex account and data

01 How to Request Deletion

To ensure the security of your data, we require a formal request to process account deletions. Please follow these steps:

STEP 1
Send an email from your registered Drex account email address to zam.contact@yahoo.com.
STEP 2
Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - [Your Username]".
STEP 3
Our support team will verify your identity and process the deletion within 7 business days.
Send Deletion Request Email

02 What Data is Deleted?

When your request is processed, the following information will be permanently removed from our active servers:

  • Your profile information (Email, Username, Bio, Profile Picture).
  • All your public posts and comments on the Drex community.
  • Your voting history and social interactions.
  • Your Drex Bank digital card and transaction history.

Note: Some transaction data may be retained for a limited period as required by financial regulations or legal obligations.

03 Data Retention

Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. We maintain a 30-day "grace period" backup for security purposes before final purging from our systems. After this period, all personal identifiers are completely erased or anonymized.
